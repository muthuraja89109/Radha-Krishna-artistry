How to Book

Please read the terms and conditions (pinned post & highlights on Instagram) before booking.

  1. Read the Terms & Conditions on Instagram, then DM to book. Pre-payment is must to confirm your slot.
  2. We will share the timing for your booking date a day before. Please confirm the date only if that timing works for you.
  3. Duration: full hand front and back takes 5–6 hrs; intricate designs can take 10–12 hrs.
  4. Group booking (2/3 persons): fix a date for the next day or previous day. More than one person on the same day may cause health issues.
  5. We always use organic cones. The stain lasts 4–6 days, so start 2–4 days before your event for the perfect colour.
